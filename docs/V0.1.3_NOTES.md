# v0.1.3 notes: the visual-first toolkit

## 1. Product rows

- **The picture leads each row.**
  - Comfortable: `clamp(64px, 20.5vw, 84px)`, which is 84px at 412px wide, 74px at 360 and 66px at 320.
  - Compact: `clamp(56px, 16.5vw, 68px)`.
  - Sizes are in px, so larger Android text grows the words, not the picture (`index.css`, `.row-thumb`).
- **Typography:**
  - name 15px, two lines;
  - flavour and size 13px;
  - stock 12px.

  All of these are in rem, so they follow the phone's font size.
- **Controls:**
  - Count is a compact button: the icon with "Count" under it, 44px or more.
  - Order mode keeps "+" and then [− n +].
  - When the text is large, the control moves under the name instead of squeezing it. Name and control share a wrapping line, and the name keeps at least 7.5rem.
- **Grid layout: not added.** A grid would cut names to one line and hide the stock line. A list with large pictures was better at the counter.

## 2. Pictures (audit and new handling)

- **Audit.** All 141 catalog products use `placehold.co` addresses: coloured labels with a caption ("AZ Fruit Punch"), not photos of packaging. The app used to fetch them over the network, so offline they showed a grey initial.
- **The tiles are now drawn on the phone** from the address itself, with the same colours and caption. They work offline and make no request. The stored `imageUrl` is unchanged.
- **No packaging images were added, invented or downloaded.**
- **Products without a picture** get a label tile: a steady colour per product and its distinguishing words (the flavour), for example "Caution Extreme Fruit…".
- **Photos from the phone.** Catalog › edit product › Picture has **Choose photo** (the system picker) and **Take photo**.
  - Both use the WebView's `<input type="file">`.
  - The app asks for nothing until the camera is actually used. `CAMERA` was already declared in v0.1.0, and no new permission was added.
  - Photos are resized to 480px JPEG and kept in IndexedDB, not in localStorage.
  - See DATA_SAFETY.md › "Added in v0.1.3" for how photos are backed up.

## 3. Navigation

- **The bottom bar is Counter · Orders · Catalog.** Settings, backup, restore, product photos, order history and updates are in the ☰ menu (header).
- **The mode switch reads "Order | Count".**
- **No camera or AI button was added.**

## 4. Bulk import (CSV)

Format and rules: `utils/csvImport.ts`. In short:

- **Required:** a header row with `name`, `supplier`. Case suppliers also need `units_per_case` and `case_cost`.
- **Optional:** `flavor`, `size`, `category`, `retail_price`, `reorder_at`, `upc`, `image_url`.
- **Prices:** never made up. A row without a case cost is flagged, not added at $0.
- **Existing products** (same UPC, or the same supplier, name, flavour and size) are listed as "already in catalog" and never changed. Repeated rows are listed as such.
- **Picture addresses** must be https; anything else is dropped with a warning.
- **The preview** shows every row. Ready rows are ticked and can be unticked. Only the approved rows are added.

**Fruit Guy and Candy Guy** show an empty state that says nothing was made up for them, with a way to Catalog. No products were invented.

## 5. Order history

- **Format.** See the header of `utils/orderHistory.ts`. It is stored in `inventory_orderHistory`, schemaVersion 1.
- **Saving.**
  - Share sheet › **Save to history** saves a snapshot: date, supplier, products with names, sizes and prices as they were, quantities, units, estimate, and the exact shared text.
  - The current order is not cleared.
  - Saving an unchanged order again adds nothing.
- **Status.** "Prepared" until the person taps **Mark as sent**. The app cannot know whether a text was delivered.
- **The history screen** is Orders › Order history, or the menu. It offers:
  - a filter by supplier;
  - open, Copy, Text;
  - **Use as current order**, which asks first and replaces only that supplier's lines; products no longer in the catalog are skipped and counted;
  - **Remove from history**, which asks first.

## 6. Backup reminder

- **The Counter screen reminds** when no backup has left the phone for 7 days, or ever. "Not now" hides it for 3 days.
- **Settings › Backup shows what is actually known:**
  - saved (Android "Save to…" confirmed);
  - download started (the browser does not confirm);
  - sent to the share menu (not confirmed where it went);
  - created but not saved;
  - failed.
- **Copying the text does not count as a backup.**
- All earlier protections (verification, checksums, restore preview, snapshots) are unchanged.

## 7. Needs attention (no AI)

On a counted supplier, a one-line card lists products:

- at or below their reorder point (verified count only);
- never counted;
- not counted for 14 days.

Review shows each one with its reason, for example "Verified 8 · reorder at 12" or "Never counted". **Count these** walks the keypad through them with Save & Next. It never suggests an order quantity.

## 8. Shelf-tag QR

- **Web version:** unchanged (`<address>#/product/<id>`).
- **Android app.** The code now carries `deliordernyc://product/<id>`.
  - An intent filter for exactly that scheme and host hands it to MainActivity.
  - The page picks it up through `DeliNative.consumeProductLink()`, at start, when resumed, and when the app is already open.
  - It then opens like a `#/product/<id>` address: an unknown id goes to Counter with a short note.
  - No permission was added. No web hosting is needed.
- **Honest limits:**
  - It opens only on a phone with DeliOrderNYC installed.
  - Some camera apps show the link as text or ask before opening an app link. Test on the Samsung camera and Google Lens.
  - Printing is still not available inside the APK.
- Supplier-order QR, Copy and Text are unchanged.

## 9. Future camera feature (architecture, not built)

The extension points from C2.3_NOTES.md §4 still apply. v0.1.3 adds the pieces a camera flow will reuse:

- `utils/photoStore.ts`: resize and local storage;
- the file-input capture path, already used by Take photo;
- `utils/attention.ts`: which products to check.

Recognition must produce *proposals* that the person confirms before `saveStockCount`. Nothing estimated is ever shown as verified.

## 10. Floating AI assistant (placement reserved, not built)

- **Placement:**
  - a 56px round button on the end side;
  - `bottom: calc(var(--nav-h) + var(--safe-bottom) + 12px)`;
  - raised above the order tray when the tray shows (`+ 4.5rem`);
  - hidden while any sheet, the menu or the keyboard is open;
  - never over the tray's Review button, Save buttons or the bottom bar.
- **The bar was not changed** to reserve room for it: no placeholder button, no permission, no key.
- **Data rules (C2.3_NOTES.md §5):**
  - answers only from verified counts;
  - no API key in the APK;
  - nothing sent without the person seeing what is sent and to whom.
