# v0.1.6 notes: compact rows, the Camera tab, and the camera photo that did not come back

## 1. The camera bug (Galaxy A17: Take Photo → back on another screen, 0 photos)

### What was wrong
v0.1.5's **Take photo** was a WebView file input with `capture="environment"`. Inside Capacitor this works as follows:
- `BridgeWebChromeClient.onShowFileChooser` starts the camera app with `ACTION_IMAGE_CAPTURE`;
- the photo is written to a file in the app's Pictures folder;
- the result is handed back through a callback **kept only in memory** (`activityListener`, plus the WebView's own `ValueCallback`).

While the camera app is open, Android may close DeliOrderNYC to free memory. It can close:
- the **activity**;
- the **WebView renderer** (the page);
- the **whole process**.

A 6 GB phone running a 50 MP camera does this often. When it happens:
- the in-memory callback no longer exists, so the photo is never delivered;
- the page starts again (at Counter, or reloads on the check);
- the check shows **0 photos**;
- the photo file is left unused in the app's Pictures folder.

This matches the A17 report exactly. A browser cannot show it: there the page is never closed while the camera is open.

### Other places checked
- **Permissions.** The camera permission was already declared; Android asks the first time. Not the cause.
- **URIs.** The `FileProvider` paths were correct. Not the cause.
- **Decoding and compression.** The 1280 px resize ran in the page, decoding a 12–50 MP photo in the renderer. That can add memory pressure (it is now done natively, see below), but it was not the main cause.
- **IndexedDB writes.** They were never reached: no file arrived.
- **React state.** The check is saved after every change. Not the cause.

### What changed
- **The camera writes into a private inbox.** `DeliNativePlugin.takeShelfPhoto` opens the camera app with `ACTION_IMAGE_CAPTURE`, writing straight into `files/shelf-inbox/`. That folder is private to the app and shared with the camera app only through `FileProvider` (`res/xml/file_paths.xml`, path `shelf_inbox`).
- **The page collects the inbox on every start and resume** (`utils/shelfCamera.ts`, `CounterShell`). The native side shrinks each finished photo to 1280 px, upright, JPEG 80 (`inSampleSize` first, so a 50 MP photo never needs 200 MB). The page stores it in IndexedDB, adds it to the check, and **only then** deletes it from the inbox. Names already stored are remembered (`deliordernyc_sys_shelfInbox`), so a photo is never added twice, even if the app is closed between storing and deleting.
- **The check is remembered before the camera opens** (`deliordernyc_sys_shelfCapture`). When the app comes back, even on another screen, it goes back to that check and says what happened:
  - "Photo saved to this check.";
  - or "The camera did not return a photo… Please take it again.";
  - or that a photo could not be read;
  - or that photos went into a new check because the check already had 12.
- **If saving fails** (storage full or blocked), the photo **stays in the inbox** and is added the next time the app opens. The person is told so.
- **Errors in words:**
  - camera not allowed (with where to allow it);
  - no camera app;
  - camera could not open.
- **Choose photos (gallery) uses the same inbox.** `chooseShelfPhotos` opens Android's photo picker (Android 13+, otherwise `GET_CONTENT`; no permission) and copies the chosen photos into the inbox.
- **No new permission.** `CAMERA` was already declared. The `@capacitor/camera` and `@capacitor/app` plugins were evaluated and not used:
  - the App plugin would take over Android Back by default;
  - the restored-result route covers activity recreation, but not a renderer that dies while the activity lives;
  - the inbox covers both.
- **Browsers** keep the file inputs: there the page is never closed by the camera.

### What is verified automatically, and what is not
- **Verified (unit tests, `utils/shelfCamera.test.ts`, 13 tests, with a fake inbox and a fresh copy of the page modules standing in for "Android reopened the app"):**
  - the photo is stored in the same check and the inbox is emptied;
  - several photos go into one check;
  - the photo is recovered after the app was closed with the camera open;
  - "no photo came back" is reported;
  - cancelling is not reported as a lost photo;
  - the inbox is left alone while the camera is still open;
  - a photo is never added twice, also when deleting it from the inbox failed;
  - only one collection runs at a time;
  - a photo with no check starts a new unfinished check;
  - the 12-photo limit spills into a new check;
  - storage failures keep the photo;
  - unreadable photos are reported;
  - the gallery goes through the same path.
- **Verified (browser tests, `e2e/v016.spec.ts`, against the real screens with a stand-in for the Android side that survives a page reload):**
  - Take photo shows the photo in the same check at once;
  - more can be added;
  - photos stay after leaving;
  - the photo comes back after a reload during the camera;
  - the app restarted on Counter goes back to the check and says the camera returned nothing.
- **Verified by CI:** the Java compiles and passes Android lint; the APK identity, permissions and version checks pass.
- **Not verified:** the phone's own camera app, Android actually closing the app, `FileProvider` grants on a real device, and the photo picker. **Not proven until the Galaxy A17 procedure below passes.**

### Galaxy A17 test procedure (once v0.1.6 is installed)
1. **Camera tab.** Tap **Camera** (bottom bar) → **New check: take photo**. Android asks for the camera the first time: allow.
2. **Take a photo.** Photograph a shelf → tap the camera's ✓ / OK. **Expected:** back in the same check (date at the top), the photo is visible in the strip, and "Photo saved to this check." shows.
3. **View it.** Tap the photo. **Expected:** it opens large. Close it.
4. **Add more.** Tap **Take photo** twice more. **Expected:** 3 photos.
5. **Choose photos.** Tap **Choose photo** → pick 2 from the gallery. **Expected:** 5 photos.
6. **Leave and return.** Tap **Counter**, then **Camera**. **Expected:** the check is under "Unfinished checks" with 5 photos, and the Camera tab shows a badge. Open it: all 5 are there.
7. **Stress the memory** (this is the bug):
   - open the check → **Take photo**;
   - in the camera, switch to the highest resolution (50 MP) if offered;
   - before taking the photo, press Home, open 3–4 heavy apps (YouTube, Chrome with tabs, a game), then return to the camera through Recents;
   - take the photo → OK.
   **Expected:** DeliOrderNYC opens on that check, possibly after a short reload, with the new photo and "Photo saved to this check." It must never show 0 photos or a different screen without the photo.
8. **Optional, the strongest test:**
   - Settings › Developer options › **Don't keep activities** ON;
   - repeat steps 2 and 5;
   - expected as above;
   - turn the option OFF afterwards.
9. **Cancel.** **Take photo** → back out of the camera without taking a photo. **Expected:** no message about a lost photo, and the photo count is unchanged.
10. **Report** for each step: pass/fail, a screenshot of any failure, and the photo count.

## 2. The layout: what was evaluated, and why rows

### The four approaches
- **A. Picture cards, two per row** (v0.1.5).
- **B. Compact rows:** 52 px picture, the flavor first, the control on the right.
- **C. Hybrid:** cards at normal text, rows at large text.
- **D. Tighter rows:** "Compact", 44 px picture, less padding.

### How they were measured
- **Catalog:** the real 141-product catalog, Arizona, Order mode.
- **Widths:** 320 / 360 / 384 / 412 px.
- **Text:** 100 / 130 / 160 / 200 %.
- **Two ways of enlarging text:**
  - **"Android"**: words grow, boxes do not. Android WebView's text size behaves like this, and it is what broke "Orde/r" on the A17.
  - **"Everything"**: boxes in rem grow too.
- **Script:** `m16.mjs` in the PR description.

### Results, Android-style text (the A17's case)

Each cell is v0.1.5 cards (A) → v0.1.6 rows (B).

- **Taps to pick five Big Can flavors:** 6 in both (one pill, then one tap each).
- **Touch targets under 44 px** at 100 % (taps that can land on the wrong thing): 54–62 → **0**.
- **Space used by the bars before the first product** (header, supplier, mode, pills, plus the navigation):
  - 100 %: 300 → 290 px;
  - 200 % Android-style: 330 → 294 px.
- **The bar also slides away while scrolling down** and comes back on any scroll up.

**With "everything" enlarged** (412 px, Big Can flavors visible): 6/6/5/2 → **10/7/7/3**. The other widths are equal or better, except 360 px at 130 %: 6 → 5. Names are now complete rather than cut, so some rows are taller.

**D (Compact)** shows the most: 12 / 13 at 412 px, 100 %. It is kept as an option in Settings › Appearance. It is not the default because its 44 px picture is less recognisable.

**C (hybrid)** would show A's numbers at normal text, where A is clearly worse than B (5 vs 9). Its only gain is a bigger picture, and the large preview (tap the picture) gives that on demand. Not chosen.

### Decision: B, rows, as the default
- **Products:** about twice as many on screen at normal text, and more at every Android text size.
- **Names:** no cut names.
- **Labels:** no split labels.
- **Taps:** 44 px targets everywhere.
- **Pictures:** still on every row (52 px), with a large preview one tap away.
- **Kept as options:** "Large pictures" keeps the v0.1.5 cards (now without "…"), and "Compact" keeps the tighter rows.

### The cost
- **Switching to a recent supplier takes 2 taps instead of 1.** The supplier is now one compact button; recent suppliers are first in its list. This gave the products the space of a whole bar.
- **Raising a quantity:** a second case takes 2 taps (✓ quantity → +) instead of 1. The first case is still one tap.

## 3. What changed on screen
- **Rows (`CounterRow`, `utils/rowText.ts`):**
  - picture, flavor first, then only what the screen does not already say: the product line without the supplier's name ("RX Energy", not "Arizona RX Energy"), and the size unless the size pill says it;
  - Boar's Head leads with the product ("Deluxe Ham", then "Sweet");
  - names wrap, never "…";
  - at the largest text the control moves under the name instead of squeezing it;
  - stored product names are unchanged.
- **Order mode:**
  - **+** adds one case (one item for item suppliers), and "Added …: 1 case(s) · Undo" appears;
  - the control becomes **✓ quantity**; tapping it opens the **quantity adjuster** (− / + / Remove from order, with Undo);
  - picking for an order never writes a count;
  - no swipe gestures (not needed; nothing depends on one).
- **Picture preview:**
  - tap the picture or the name: a large picture, the full name, size, case size, cost estimate, stock and Count;
  - colour labels say they are not a photo of the package.
- **Count mode:**
  - **Count with photos** comes first;
  - each row's **Count** opens the keypad, which is more compact: −1/+1 beside the number, one row less, the help line removed;
  - Save & Next, Undo and the verified-count rules are unchanged.
- **Top area:**
  - one supplier button and Order | Count on one row, then one row of pills;
  - the "All" button and the recent chips are gone (they are in the supplier list);
  - it slides away when scrolling down;
  - at the largest text on small phones (more than 30 % of the screen), it scrolls away instead and choosing a pill jumps to the products;
  - the chosen pill and the place in the list are remembered as before.
- **Labels never break inside a word (`FitText`):**
  - Order | Count and the tab names get slightly smaller text (to 70 %) instead of "Orde/r";
  - if a label still does not fit, it wraps only between words.
- **Bottom navigation:** Counter | Orders | **Camera** | Catalog. Camera opens shelf photos, and its badge counts unfinished checks. Settings, backup and profile stay in the menu (☰).
- **Shelf photos:**
  - **New check: take photo** and **Choose photos** first;
  - unfinished checks with a photo and a count;
  - in a check, Take and Choose lead the photo strip;
  - the long explanations moved behind ⓘ (How it works);
  - review rows, confirmed totals and Undo are unchanged;
  - no recognition is claimed.

## 4. Camera → review → inventory → order suggestions (prepared, not built)
- **The chain:** photo (inbox → check) → proposed rows → the person confirms totals → verified counts.
- **The slot for a future recognizer:** it would add rows with its own `source` and a confidence (`utils/shelfCheck.ts`). It goes through the same review and confirmation. Visible is never total.
- **Order suggestions:** they would read the verified counts against each product's reorder point (the Stock review already lists low and out). No AI, accounts or billing were started.

## 5. Data safety
- **Unchanged:** main data keys, the backup format, product ids and names, verified counts, orders, history, photos, shelf checks and their 14-day retention.
- **New, device-only `deliordernyc_sys_` keys:** `shelfCapture` (the check the camera is open for) and `shelfInbox` (photo names stored but not yet deleted from the inbox). They are never exported or restored.
- **New private folder:** `files/shelf-inbox/` (photos waiting to be stored; emptied as soon as they are). It is not in backups.
- **New display option:** `density: 'pictures'`. Older versions read it as Comfortable.
- **Installs over v0.1.5 and older** with nothing to migrate. Application ID, signing, WebView origin and permissions are unchanged.
